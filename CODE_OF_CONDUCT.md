# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

AG AdliAkademi is an open-source learning and development project. We want participation to be welcoming, constructive, respectful, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give constructive feedback and explain technical criticism clearly.
- Assume good faith while remaining open to correction.
- Respect different experience levels; beginners are welcome.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Respect privacy and intellectual property.
- Use only authorized and synthetic test data.
- Share cybersecurity and digital forensics knowledge responsibly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or hateful language.
- Publishing another person's private information without consent.
- Sharing credentials, private records, or real investigation evidence.
- Deliberately disrupting project discussions or testing.
- Encouraging unauthorized access or harmful activity.
- Misrepresenting test results or making unsupported vulnerability claims.

## Scope

This Code applies to project spaces, including GitHub Issues, pull requests, discussions, and community spaces when participating in AG AdliAkademi.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer privately through the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Avoid posting personal details publicly.

Reports will be reviewed as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible. The maintainer may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary.

## Enforcement

Responses may include a warning, a request to edit or remove content, temporary restriction, or removal from project spaces, depending on the circumstances.

Thank you for helping maintain a respectful community.
